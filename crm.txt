Admin
•	Create and manage employees
•	Create Sales Managers and Sales Executives
•	Assign employees under respective managers
•	Manage products/services
•	Manage CRM settings
•	Monitor complete business activities
•	View reports and dashboards
•	Receive TAT breach notifications
Operation Head
•	View approved leads
•	Verify and manage customer documents
•	Assign tasks to respective employees/departments
•	Monitor task progress
•	Approve completed tasks
•	Assign the next workflow stage
•	Monitor TAT
•	Manage payment follow-ups
•	Add payment-related remarks
Sales Manager
•	Manage Sales Executives assigned under them
•	Receive newly created leads
•	Review and approve leads
•	Verify uploaded documents
•	Monitor sales team activities
•	Track quotations and customer status
•	Monitor payment status
•	Add payment/TAT remarks
Sales Executive
•	Create new customer leads
•	Submit customer information
•	Upload required documents
•	Create quotations
•	Select products/services
•	Share quotation with customers through WhatsApp
•	Track lead status
•	View assigned tasks
•	Add required remarks
Other Staff / Department Users
The Admin/Operation Head can create users such as:
•	Computer Operator
•	Registration Team
•	Bank Coordinator
•	Installation Team
•	Other operational staff
